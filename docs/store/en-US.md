## Ausführliche Beschreibung (2103/4000 Zeichen)

```text
A small town, a few computers, one mail server – and it is 1995, ISDN is brand new. Swipe to lay a cable. Every device sends little data packets to its server and waits for the answer. When packets pile up, a red ring fills: keep your network overloaded for too long and the game is over.

Week by week the town grows, and so does technology. ISDN gives way to DSL, coax and fiber. Smartphones, game consoles, smart TVs, security cameras and smart home hubs move in, along with gaming, streaming, video calls and nightly cloud backups. Every service needs its bandwidth and has its own ping limit: a phone call forgives more than an online game.

Place routers as hubs, put up Wi-Fi access points and cell towers, pick radio channels that don’t interfere, and upgrade servers all the way to a data center. And watch out for the excavator – it loves cutting cables.

• Calm, clear strategy puzzle game in isometric style
• Travel through 30 years of network tech: ISDN, DSL, coax, fiber, Wi-Fi, 4G and 5G
• Seven services, each with its own color and shape, ten device types
• Five sceneries: River Town, Metropolis, Island & Harbor, Mountain Village, Future 2030
• Daily challenge: the same map and a rule of the day for everyone, with a streak
• 39 achievements and missions, unlockable cable skins and color themes
• Modes: Normal, Endless (no game over) and Creative (build freely)
• Turn and zoom the map with two fingers, flat overview at the tap of a button
• A choice every week: budget, routers, a server voucher or radio tech
• Incidents like excavators and power cuts keep you on your toes
• Leaderboards and cloud save with Google Play Games (optional)
• Share your network as a picture with score and year
• Short interactive tutorial, colorblind palette, large text, TalkBack in the menus
• Plays fully offline, no sign-in needed
• Available in 12 languages

Mini Networks is free to play. An ad occasionally appears between two games – never while you play. A one-time “Remove ads” purchase makes them go away. Extra sceneries unlock by playing, or can be bought on their own or as a pack.
```
