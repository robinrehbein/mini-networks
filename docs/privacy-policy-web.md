## Mini Networks – Überblick

Mini Networks ist ein Android-Spiel ohne eigenes Benutzerkonto, ohne eigene Server und ohne eigene Analyse- oder Absturzmeldedienste. Personenbezogene Daten verarbeiten nur die folgenden Dienste von Google: Werbung (Google AdMob mit der User Messaging Platform), In-App-Käufe (Google Play Billing), die optionalen Google Play Spiele-Dienste (Erfolge, Bestenlisten, Cloud-Speicherstand) und die Bewertungsanfrage von Google Play (In-App Review). Das Spiel ist ohne Konto und ohne Internet vollständig spielbar.

## Mini Networks – lokale Spieldaten

Spielstand, Einstellungen, Bestwerte je Szenerie und Modus, Spielstatistiken für Erfolge, die Serie und der Tagesbestwert der Tagesaufgabe, lokal gespeicherte Kaufberechtigungen, ein Zähler der beendeten Partien (für die Werbe-Häufigkeit) und der Zeitpunkt der letzten Bewertungsanfrage liegen auf Ihrem Gerät; ich erhalte diese Daten nicht. Wenn Sie Ihr Netz als Bild teilen, legt die App das Bild im App-Cache ab und gibt es nur an die App weiter, die Sie im Android-Teilen-Menü auswählen. Je nach Ihren Android-Einstellungen können diese App-Daten über die Android-Datensicherung in Ihrem Google-Konto gesichert werden. Sie können lokale Daten in den Android-Einstellungen löschen, indem Sie die App-Daten löschen oder die App deinstallieren.

## Mini Networks – overview

Mini Networks is an Android game without user accounts, servers, analytics or crash reporting of its own. Personal data is processed only by these Google services: ads (Google AdMob with the User Messaging Platform), in-app purchases (Google Play Billing), the optional Google Play Games services (achievements, leaderboards, cloud save) and Google Play's rating prompt (In-App Review). The game is fully playable without an account and without internet.

## Mini Networks – data on your device

Your saved game, settings, best scores per scenery and mode, game statistics for achievements, the daily challenge streak and best score of the day, locally stored purchase entitlements, a count of finished games (for the ad frequency) and when the app last asked for a rating stay on your device; I do not receive this data. If you share your network as a picture, the app puts the picture in its cache and hands it only to the app you pick in the Android share menu. Depending on your Android settings, this app data may be backed up to your Google account by Android backup. You can delete local data in the Android settings by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

## Mini Networks – ads and consent

The free version may show ads between games and, only if you explicitly choose it, a rewarded video. For this the app uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. The ads SDK may send Google in particular your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app identifiers including the Android advertising ID, interactions with ads and technical diagnostics. Google uses this data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Personalised ads and storing or reading information on your device are based, where required, on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Where legally permitted, non-personalised ads and security purposes are based on my legitimate interest in financing and securing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice through UMP. You can change or withdraw your choice in the app's privacy settings. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in the system settings.

After buying "Remove ads" no more ads are loaded; from the next start on, the app no longer initialises the ads SDK. More on Google's processing: [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Google may process data outside the European Economic Area; Google's privacy notices describe the transfer mechanisms.

## Mini Networks – in-app purchases

Optional purchases such as "Remove ads" and additional sceneries are handled by Google Play. You enter payment details with Google; I do not receive them. The app receives product information and a purchase token from Google Play to verify and acknowledge the purchase, and stores the entitlement on your device. The legal basis for my processing is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies to Google's processing.

## Mini Networks – Google Play Games services (optional)

Achievements, leaderboards and the cloud save use Google Play Games services (Google Ireland Limited). If you have a Play Games profile on your device, Android signs you in automatically; without a profile or internet the game works without these features. Google then processes your player ID and Play Games profile (gamertag, avatar; you choose its visibility in Play Games), unlocked achievements, scores for the leaderboards of the sceneries and the daily challenge, and the cloud save with your game statistics, best scores, daily streak and best score of the day (not the running game). The app only receives this game data of your profile for this game; I do not receive it. The purpose is the features you use (achievements, leaderboards, progress across devices); the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can delete this data through your [Play Games profile](https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google account. The [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Mini Networks – rating prompt

After a good moment, such as a new best score, the app may show Google Play's rating dialog (In-App Review), at most every 30 days. Google Play decides whether it appears. You rate and review directly with Google Play; the app does not learn whether or how you rated. The [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
